# Sound credits

No third-party audio is shipped: no samples, recordings or downloaded sounds.

- The effects, ambience and voices: synthesized at runtime in `web/play/audio.js`, the
  project's own.
- The music in `bgm/`: the project's own tracks, rendered on
  [Surge XT](https://surge-synthesizer.github.io/) 1.3.4 (GPL-3.0, by the Surge Synth Team)
  factory patches in [DawDreamer](https://github.com/DBraun/DawDreamer) 0.9.0 (GPL-3.0, by
  David Braun), by `scripts/sound/bgm.py`. Neither is shipped; the renders are ours.
- The agent's voice reads Chinese with [pinyin-pro](https://github.com/zh-lx/pinyin-pro) 3.29.4
  (MIT, by zh-lx), whose ESM build is shipped in `web/play/vendor/pinyin-pro/` with its
  `LICENSE`.

A CC-BY asset added later gets its credit line here: title, author, URL, licence.

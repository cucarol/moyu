# Fonts

Fusion Pixel Font, 12px proportional, TTF-flavoured woff2, release 2026.10.25, by TakWolf. https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font. SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL.txt`). The page loads one build, picked by its language in `i18n.js`:

- `fusion-pixel-12px-zh_hans.woff2`: Simplified Chinese, and the Latin and Cyrillic languages (en, es, fr, de, pt-BR, ru).
- `fusion-pixel-12px-ja.woff2`: Japanese.
- `fusion-pixel-12px-ko.woff2`: Korean.
- `fusion-pixel-12px-zh_hant.woff2`: Traditional Chinese (zh-TW).

Each build merges glyphs from Ark Pixel Font (TakWolf), Cubic 11 (ACh-K) and Galmuri (Lee Minseo), all under the OFL 1.1; their notices are in `LICENSES/`, as shipped in the release.
